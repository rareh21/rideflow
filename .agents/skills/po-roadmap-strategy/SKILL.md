# Product Owner Strategy & Uber Benchmarking Skill

## Overview
This skill guides the Product Owner in defining commercial outcomes, approving feature scopes, and positioning RideFlow to compete against Uber, Lyft, and premium mobility providers (e.g. Uber Black, Wheely).

## Benchmarking Protocol Against Uber

### 1. Vehicle Tier Segmentation
Ensure RideFlow maintains explicit separation of tiers:
- **RideFlow GO**: High-frequency, cost-conscious city trips (economy hatchbacks & compact sedans).
- **RideFlow PLUS / Comfort**: Newer vehicles, top-rated drivers, extra legroom, temperature preferences.
- **RideFlow Black & Lux**: Luxury executive sedans (Mercedes E-Class, BMW 5/7, Audi A6/A8), professional dress code, luggage assistance, quiet ride guarantee.
- **RideFlow XL**: 6+ passenger SUVs & vans for groups and airport luggage.

### 2. Monetization & Unit Economics Check
When evaluating any feature, verify:
- **Take Rate**: Platform commission structure (typically 20-25% standard, 28% on Black/Lux).
- **Surge Mechanics**: Dynamic surge pricing to incentivize driver positioning during rain, rush hour, or special events.
- **Driver Retention**: Instant cashout fees ($0.50-$1.00 per instant transfer) as ancillary revenue.
- **Cancellation Economics**: Fee split ($5 fee with 75% to driver) if rider cancels >2 minutes after match.

## Milestone Verification & Review Checklist
- [ ] Business outcome clearly articulated with measurable KPIs (e.g. driver acceptance rate >85%, pickup ETA <4.5 min).
- [ ] No feature creeping without corresponding revenue or retention justification.
- [ ] Alignment with AGENTS.md definition of done before milestone closure.
