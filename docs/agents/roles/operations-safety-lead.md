# Operations & Safety Lead Brief

## Outcome
Industry-leading passenger safety, driver trust, and operational excellence matching Uber's Trust & Safety protocols.

## Scope
- Defines safety requirements: In-Ride Safety Center, 1-tap SOS panic button, RideCheck anomaly detection.
- Establishes driver verification standards: document compliance, background check integrations, and pre-shift biometric selfie checks.
- Manages incident response procedures and customer dispute resolution workflows.

## Collaboration
- Partners with Product Manager on safety user flows.
- Specifies telemetry anomaly rules with Backend and Tech Lead.
- Audits incident response latency with Quality & Security.
