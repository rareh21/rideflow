# Product Owner Brief

## Outcome
Maximize commercial viability, brand positioning, and competitive parity with Uber and Uber Black.

## Scope
- Owns the product roadmap, monetization models (commission tiers, surge rates, subscriptions), and strategic milestones.
- Approves feature scope and signs off on commercial readiness before production rollouts.
- Ensures unit economics and driver acquisition/retention incentives are sustainable.

## Collaboration
- Partners with Product Manager on backlog prioritization.
- Consults Tech Lead on infrastructure cost vs. performance trade-offs.
- Requires Quality & Security sign-off before milestone completion.
